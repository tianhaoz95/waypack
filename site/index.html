How it works

Planning happens where you already think: in your AI agent. Waypack turns the plan into something you can use on a mountain road with zero bars.

Ask your agent

“Plan 3 days in Sequoia over Christmas with a toddler, using Waypack.” It asks a few questions, researches, geocodes every place and computes real routes.

It publishes a trip bundle

A self-contained mobile guide (itinerary, lodging, food, safety, packing, backup plans) plus an offline vector map cut to your trip area.

Download, then go

Open Waypack and tap Download while you have signal. Everything (pages, map, your location) works in airplane mode.

Built for no-service places

A real offline map

OpenStreetMap trails, roads and labels for your trip area, your planned routes drawn on top, and the blue dot from your phone's GPS. No signal needed.

As detailed as your agent

Not a fixed form. Your agent writes the guide: why this order, where the last gas is, what to do if the road closes, what a toddler needs at 7,000 ft.

Navigate with your maps app

Every place has a Navigate button that hands off to Google Maps or Apple Maps. Download their offline area too, and turn-by-turn works as well.

Native Today view

The app reads the trip's structured data to show what's now and next, even if a guide's layout is unusual.

Reminders before you go

If a trip starts in 3 days and isn't downloaded yet, Waypack reminds you while you still have signal.

Private by default

Trips are private to your account, encrypted in transit, and reachable only through short-lived links. No ads, no tracking in your trip pages.

Connect your agent

Waypack is a remote MCP server with OAuth sign-in. Use the same email as the app. For the best plans, also install the Waypack skill. It teaches your agent the interview, coverage checklist and bundle format.

Add the server, then install the skill:

claude mcp add --transport http waypack https://waypack.app/mcp
npx @waypack/cli skill install

Then ask: Plan a weekend in Lake Tahoe in January with Waypack. Sign in with your Waypack email when Claude opens the browser. Claude Code writes the bundle to ./waypack/<trip>/, validates it, and uploads it.

Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector, and paste:

https://waypack.app/mcp

No skill needed: the agent calls get_authoring_guide and uploads with upload_bundle_inline.

Any client that supports remote MCP over Streamable HTTP with OAuth:

{
  "mcpServers": {
    "waypack": { "url": "https://waypack.app/mcp" }
  }
}

Headless or no OAuth? Create a personal API token in the app (Settings → API tokens) and send it as Authorization: Bearer wpk_….

FAQ

Which AI agents work with Waypack?

Any MCP client that supports remote servers: Claude Code, Claude (web, desktop, mobile) custom connectors, Cursor, and others. The skill improves results in agents that support Agent Skills.

Does the map really work without signal?

Yes, on Pro. When you tap Download, Waypack saves a vector map of your trip area (trails, roads, labels) to your phone. Your location comes from GPS, which doesn't need cell service.

Does Waypack do turn-by-turn navigation?

No. Tap Navigate to hand off to Google Maps or Apple Maps. For offline turn-by-turn, also download their offline area (your trip's checklist reminds you).

Can I edit a trip in the app?

Edits happen in your agent: “move the Sherman Tree visit to the morning”. It re-publishes and the app shows Update available.

Where does the map data come from?

OpenStreetMap, via the Protomaps basemap. © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database License.

How does billing work?

Subscriptions are handled on the web by Stripe from your account page, where you can also change your card, download invoices or cancel. The mobile apps are free and never charge you. Cancelling keeps Pro until the end of the paid period; trips you've published stay yours.

What happens to my data?

See the privacy policy. Short version: your trips are private to your account, we don't sell data, and deleting your account deletes your trips and maps.
