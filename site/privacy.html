Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-12-01 · Draft for review before launch

Waypack (“we”) is operated by Hejitech. This policy explains what we collect when you use the Waypack apps, the Waypack MCP server used by AI agents, and waypack.app.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent your data, and we don't use it for advertising.
  • Trip pages run on your phone with no analytics or trackers, and can't send data to the internet.
  • Your location (GPS) stays on your device; it's used only to draw the blue dot on your map.
  • We don't train AI models on your trips.

Your AI agent

Your agent (e.g. Claude, Cursor) is a separate service with its own privacy terms. Waypack only receives what the agent sends to the Waypack MCP server while you're signed in.

Where data lives

Account and trip records: Supabase (Postgres). Files: Cloudflare R2. Server code and website: Cloudflare Workers. Payments: Stripe. These providers process data on our behalf under their data-processing terms. Data may be processed in the United States.

Security

Trips are private to your account and reachable only through short-lived signed links. On your phone, trips are served only to the Waypack app over a local, token-protected connection.

Your choices

  • Delete a trip: ask your agent (delete_trip).
  • Delete your account: on your account page or in the app (Settings → Delete account). This cancels any subscription and deletes your account, trips and maps from our servers. You can also do this from your account page on waypack.app.
  • Access or export: email privacy@waypack.app.

Children

Waypack isn't directed to children under 13 and we don't knowingly collect their data. Trips may mention children's ages when you include them in a plan; that information is stored as part of your trip.

Changes

We'll post changes here and update the date above. Material changes will be announced in the app.
