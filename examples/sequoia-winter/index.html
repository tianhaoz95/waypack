Overview

Three relaxed days in Sequoia's Giant Forest over Christmas: sledding at Wolverton, the General Sherman Tree, and easy walks among the giant sequoias, with a nap break every afternoon. Two nights at Wuksachi Lodge, the only lodging open in the Giant Forest area in winter.

Conditions & live checks

Winter in the Giant Forest (6,400–7,300 ft) usually means snow on the ground and nights well below freezing. Storms can close or slow Generals Highway for hours, and tire chains can be required at any time — carry them even with AWD/4WD (it's the rule, not a suggestion). Holiday week is one of the busiest of the year: expect lines at the entrance and full parking lots by late morning.

Conditions change daily. Check these before you leave home and whenever you get Wi-Fi at the lodge — nothing in this guide replaces them.

Emergency & safety

No-service zones

Assume no signal from the Ash Mountain entrance until you're back in Three Rivers, except near Wuksachi Lodge and Lodgepole (Wi-Fi/landline). Tell the front desk your plan if you head out in a storm.

Winter driving

  • Practice fitting the chains at home once — doing it first time on ice with a toddler in the car is miserable.
  • Downhill: low gear, not brakes. Use pullouts to let faster traffic pass.
  • Shaded curves stay icy all day, especially after 3pm.

Cold & altitude

Toddlers lose heat fast. Watch for shivering, fussiness, pale or clumsy hands. Warm up in the car every 30 minutes of snow play, keep them dry, and snack often. Sunburn is easy at 7,000 ft on snow — sunscreen and sunglasses.

With a toddler

  • Carrier > stroller on snow. The Sherman Tree main trail has stairs.
  • Naps: 1–3pm at the lodge each day. The drive up is a good morning nap too.
  • Carsickness: the climb from Foothills has ~130 curves. Light breakfast, window cracked, stop at a pullout if needed.
  • Meals: The Peaks is the only sit-down option in winter. Pack a cooler with toddler-friendly food for lunches and emergencies.
  • Sledding: feet-first, on your lap. Stay away from the road and parking lot.

Backup plans

Dec 24 — storm or chain controls on the way up

Wait it out in Three Rivers (coffee, River View Grill), call the road line, and go up when it clears. If Generals Highway closes, rebook the first night in Three Rivers and drive up Dec 25 morning.

Dec 25 — Wolverton road not plowed yet

Swap the morning and afternoon: General Sherman (accessible lot on Generals Hwy is plowed first) in the morning, sledding after the nap. The snowy meadow by the Giant Forest Museum is fine for gentle play (not sledding).

Too cold / toddler unwell

Short outings only: the Sherman Tree from the accessible lot, lodge fireplace, early dinner. If symptoms are serious, call 911 or drive down to Kaweah Health in Visalia (≈ 1.5–2 h).

Dec 26 — storm forecast

Skip the morning snow session and leave right after breakfast so you're below the snow line before it starts.
